A few things to know

Who can use PastorPro?

PastorPro is designed for pastors, church treasurers and mission administrators. Workspace access depends on your account approval, role and organizational assignments. Contact your mission administrator if you need an account or an assignment change.

Does this page show real ministry records?

No. The workspace preview above uses illustrative sample information. Your account records are accessed only through the WebApp.

How is my information handled?

PastorPro uses Firebase for authentication and cloud records. Reporting is organized by account roles and assignments, and submitted reports may be reviewed by authorized church and mission personnel. The app’s Privacy Policy and Account Deletion pages explain its data practices, including retention of church reports with account attribution removed after deletion. Contact your mission administrator for privacy assistance.
